About VetFlipsCo

VetFlipsCo is a veteran-owned shop building practical spreadsheet tools for people whose lives run on deadlines, ratings, and fine print. Every workbook does one job well: the math is real, the formulas show their work, and the yellow cells are the only ones you ever have to touch.

Our tools are built for resellers pricing their next flip, veterans tracking VA claims and combined ratings, service members planning a TSP and pension, homeowners keeping warranties and maintenance from quietly lapsing, and pilots watching currency deadlines. There are no subscriptions, no apps to install, and no macros. You simply get a file you own, in Excel or Google Sheets.

Every workbook ships with labeled sample data so you can watch it work before you trust it with your own numbers. And when rates, limits, or rules change, buyers get the updated version.

Built once, and built carefully, by people who needed these tools themselves.
