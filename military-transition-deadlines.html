Military Transition Deadlines: The Windows That Close Quietly

Your separation date never moves, but almost nobody hands you the full list of deadlines that hang off it. Miss the BDD window and your VA claim starts months later. Miss the VGLI window and life insurance gets a health exam and a higher price. Here are the windows and when they run, along with what it costs if one lapses.

The leave decision is a money decision

Terminal leave has a price tag most people never compute. Taking leave pays base pay plus BAH and BAS for every day. Selling it back pays base pay only — monthly base pay ÷ 30 × days — and you're capped at 60 days sold across a career.

The sample run from our planner

A 20-year retiree with 60 days of leave on the books at separation: 45 days taken as terminal leave pays $10,365 in total compensation across those days; 15 days sold back pays $2,300. Same leave balance, two very different checks. The right split depends on when the next paycheck starts, and that is the math nobody does on a whiteboard in the TAP classroom.

Why a checklist on paper fails

TAP gives you the tasks. What it doesn't give you is a countdown: every one of these dates derives from a single number, your separation date, and they arrive in an order that feels wrong (your VA claim window closes before your final out-processing even starts). A static list can't flag "BDD closes in 19 days." A spreadsheet that knows your separation date can, because every due date is a formula, not a sticky note.

Questions service members ask about transition deadlines

When should I file my VA claim if I am still on active duty?

Through Benefits Delivery at Discharge (BDD): the window opens 180 days before separation and closes 90 days out. Miss it and you file after separation instead, which starts the claim clock at zero and adds months before a decision.

How long does SGLI last after I separate?

SGLI coverage runs 120 days after separation. To convert to VGLI without health questions, apply within 240 days of separation; the final conversion deadline is 1 year plus 120 days, and after the 240-day mark underwriting applies.

Is it better to take terminal leave or sell it back?

Taking leave pays base pay plus BAH and BAS for every day. Selling back pays base pay only — monthly base pay divided by 30, times the days — and is capped at 60 days sold across a career. In our planner's sample, 45 days taken paid $10,365 against $2,300 for 15 days sold; the right split depends on when your next paycheck starts.

What is TAMP?

The Transitional Assistance Management Program: 180 days of transitional healthcare coverage after separation for eligible service members and their families, bridging the gap before civilian insurance starts.
